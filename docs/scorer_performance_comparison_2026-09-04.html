Three-scorer predictive-performance comparison

On contextual child speech, Mistral-7B has the lowest child-balanced BPC (2.137); the ordering is Mistral-7B < Qwen3-14B < TinyDialogues-135M.

This is an exact-paired comparison of Mistral-7B-v0.3, Qwen3-14B, and TinyDialogues-135M. Lower Unicode bits per character means less surprise on these targets.

The ranking is evaluation-domain specific. It is not a general ranking of semantics, child knowledge, or usefulness, and per-model-token perplexity is not used across tokenizers.

Main answer

  • Qwen3-14B is more surprised than Mistral in all seven evaluated conditions.
  • TinyDialogues-135M is less surprised without context but more surprised in every contextual condition.
