How to read the evidence labels

Every executive card carries a visible label and interpretation text; color is only a secondary cue.

  • Completed / supportedThe analysis is complete and the frozen evidence supports the registered reading.
  • QualifiedThe result is informative, but a stated gate limits its interpretation or promotion.
  • ContraryThe observed direction runs opposite to the registered prediction.
  • DescriptiveThe comparison is explicitly non-causal and separate from longitudinal confirmation.
  • PendingThe required evidence or validation is not yet complete.

Five-result executive summary

These cards intentionally keep supported, qualified, contrary, descriptive, and pending evidence distinct.
